Desk simulation: before = main.py as committed in 356d010 (ran on the robot), after = current main.py.
2D model (default): sonar beam +/-10 deg, echoes lost past 25 deg incidence; encoder slip on the back face
makes the position estimate lag or lead, like the 300-780 mm seen on the robot.
body = true distance of the robot's axle from where it started.  max wall = worst true measuring-point
distance from the obstacle while following.  >30cm = seconds past the 30 cm rule.
Files: <scenario>_<before|after>.txt (console), .csv (run log as saved on the brick), _truth.csv (true path);
paths_slanted_before.png / paths_slanted_after.png = true paths on slanted zigzag fronts.

Scenarios: a short front wall | b inside corner 300 mm east | c estimate ahead, hit near west end | d plain box |
f long wall | g 250 mm front wall | h inside corner 150 mm east | z1 sawtooth east face | z2 staircase back face |
z3 deep sawtooth | s1-s5 the front is a zigzag of 300 mm panels sloped +/-20 deg and the robot hits a slanted one
(s1/s2 sloping down to the east, s3/s4 rising, s5 next to a notch).  'lag' = the estimate drifts ~450-800 mm.
