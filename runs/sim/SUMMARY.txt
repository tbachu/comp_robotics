Desk simulation of main.py (old = main.py before the lap-end fix, new = current main.py).
2D kinematic model: sonar beam +/-10 deg, echoes lost past 25 deg incidence; encoder slip on the
obstacle's back face injected to make odometry X lag (or lead), like the 300-780 mm seen on the robot.
Start = measuring point at (2000, 500). 'miss' = true distance from the start when the run ended.
"sim" counts the ~14 s spent waiting for CENTER; the task time is the RESULT line's ms.
Per run: <scenario>_<old|new>.txt (console), _new.csv (run log, same as on the brick), _truth.csv (true path).

Scenarios: a short front wall, hit 200 mm from its outside corner, X lags | b front wall ends in an inside
corner 300 mm east, X lags | c X runs ahead, hit 100 mm from the wall's west end | d baseline, no slip |
e no obstacle (checks a STOPPED run still logs) | f long wall, X lags | g 250 mm front wall, X lags |
h inside corner 150 mm east, X ahead
